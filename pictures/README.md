# pictures — source material for an icon or logo

**Not a chosen mark. Nothing here has been adopted, and no decision has been
made.** These are raw candidates kept so that a later pass at an application
icon or logo starts from something rather than a blank page.

Both land on the same idea, and it is the one the project is named for:
[README](../README.md) and [About.txt](../About.txt) take **Väinämöinen** — the
Kalevala's bearded "eternal sage" and "singing sorcerer", born old and wise —
as the whole concept behind an instant-on player that chooses the music itself.
A grey-bearded wizard is that figure; putting him behind a pair of decks is the
"digital shaman" idea About.txt describes, drawn literally.

## If either is taken forward

Recorded now because both points are easy to miss until late, and expensive
then.

- **`DJ.png` is the one built for this job.** A limited palette, hard black
  outline and a strong silhouette are what survive being scaled to 32 px or
  16 px in a browser tab. The turntables read as a shape even when the detail
  is gone. It carries a **generator glyph in the bottom-right corner** that
  would have to be removed before any real use.
- **`Wizard.png` will not scale.** Its charm is entirely in the detail —
  bottles, book spines, dried herbs — and all of it turns to mush below about
  128 px. It is a *mood* reference, not an icon candidate: usable to settle
  colour and character, then redrawn.
- **Both appear to be AI-generated.** That is fine for exploring a direction
  and not enough for a mark that ends up on a release, a store listing, or
  anything trademarked. Whatever gets adopted should be redrawn deliberately,
  with its provenance and rights known, rather than shipped from these files.
- **Neither is a 1:1 ratio problem yet**, but both are square, and a logo that
  also has to work as a wordmark or a wide header has not been thought about
  at all.
