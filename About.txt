## 🎵 The Name: Your Favourite, Without Being Asked
"Lempi" is poetic-archaic Finnish for love, from a Proto-Finnic root that
probably meant heat or burning. In compounds it is the ordinary Finnish prefix
for "favourite": lempilaulu is a favourite song, lempimusiikki favourite music,
lempilaulaja a favourite singer. For a player whose entire job is learning which
passages this listener loves and putting them into the stream, that is the most
exact word available in the language — and it is opaque in English, where it
reads simply as a soft two-syllable name.

Two other threads run through the same word. It is the root of lemmikki, the
everyday Finnish word for a pet animal. And the Kalevala connection is in the
family tree rather than bolted on: Lemminkäinen, the reckless singer-hero of
Sibelius's four legends, is son of Lempi. The library builder this player
depends on, Vipunen, takes its name from the same epic.

## ⚡ Instant Power-Up
A music player should not make you wait to hear music. Lempi is built so that
the first passage starts at power-on, not after a loading screen, a scan, or a
network handshake. On the embedded appliances this runs on, getting audio out
of the speaker is the first thing that happens; the web interface and the
management services come up afterwards, asynchronously, as the network becomes
available. Nothing that can be deferred is allowed to delay sound.

## 📻 Automated Selection
The player is not a playlist. Instead of asking the listener to scroll through
menus and assemble an order, it reads the context of the moment — stated
preferences, play history, time of day, day of week, day of year — alongside
high-level audio characteristics of the passages themselves, and builds a
continuous stream from that.

The two ideas are the same idea. A player that knows your favourites is only
useful if it is already playing them when you walk into the room, and a player
that starts instantly is only pleasant if what it starts is something you wanted
to hear.
