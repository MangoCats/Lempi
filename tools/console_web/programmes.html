Programmes

The hub's library of programmes [SPEC062]: each a named, complete day of time slots and their seeds, kept with every version it has had. A node is assigned one here. A phone asks for it when it next can — when Lempi starts and when its Mesh screen opens — and the intake must be running for it to ask. A player is handed it at once, signed, and again by the console at start and hourly until it has it. Either way the person there is asked whether to replace what the node runs, and nothing changes until they answer. Keeping their own is an answer too.
