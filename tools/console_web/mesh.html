Members

The devices this hub has enrolled in its mesh [SPEC049]. A device asks to join from its own screen — on a phone, Lempi's menu, "Mesh membership…" — naming this computer and the intake's members' port. It then appears below with a six-digit code. Accept it only if the device shows the same code: that is what proves the device you are looking at is the one asking. It must be confirmed on the device too. The intake (tools/intake.py) must be running to answer.

No mesh yet. Creating one makes this computer its hub and its authority: a key that signs who belongs. Back up data/mesh/ — losing its key means enrolling every member again.

Peers

Named installations this library can compare its catalog against [SPEC035]. Activating a peer here is the same as typing its address into the flags page's "sync with a remote" field — those three jobs keep working exactly as they do today, against whichever peer was activated last.

Leave the second address blank for every peer that hasn't split its database [IMPL002 §7.4] — an unsplit peer needs only the first. Naming which of yours are split belongs in your own notes, not here: this page outlives any particular roster.

Diff against a peer

Compares files, recordings and passages by content identity — never a full database copy [SPEC-MESH-030]. Nothing moves on its own: this only reports what differs, and a bundle for what's missing on either side is still your own export_bundle.py command, printed below rather than run for you [SPEC-SUI-110].
