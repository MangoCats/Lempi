Music sent for induction

What a Lempi node sent through tools/intake.py waits here until you decide [REQ-AND-289]. Listen, identify, then choose one of three: induct places it in the music folder by its own tags, as artist/album/, and runs the usual induction; reject sets it aside, and the intake declines it if it is offered again; repair is for a damaged copy of a file the library already holds, and offers the sender the good copy, which the sender fetches next time it asks Vipunen.
