Preference Tuning

Anywhere you see an underlined song title or artist name — in the now-playing area, the queue, the "It beat" list, or the play-history table — clicking it opens the preference panel for that exact recording or that exact artist. This is the one place you can directly tell Lempi how you feel about something, rather than leaving it to figure that out from what you skip or let play.

This panel exists in the Lempi and MuLibPlay skins. It is not present in WinAmp, so names there are plain text, not links.

The three dials

Each dial is a slider, and each one answers a different question — they are independent of each other, not one combined "popularity" score:

  • Cooldown — how long this has to wait before it's even eligible to play again. Slide it left and it can repeat sooner; slide it right and it has to wait longer. This is a hard rule: while the cooldown is running, the song simply will not be picked.
  • Recovery — once the cooldown ends, the song doesn't instantly become fully likely again — it eases back in over this length of time. A short recovery means it's back to normal quickly after its cooldown; a long one means it takes a while to feel "fully available" again even once it's technically eligible.
  • Preference — how much you generally want to hear this, independent of timing. Slide it toward "more" for a favorite you want in heavier rotation; slide it toward "less" to hear something less often without banishing it outright. All the way to "less" and it effectively stops being picked at all.

Each slider reads out roughly what it means in plain terms as you move it (a length of time for Cooldown/Recovery, a "how many times as often" figure for Preference) — the middle of each slider is always the Program Director's own default for whatever you're tuning (a recording or an artist have different defaults from each other), and moving toward either edge changes things faster the further you go, so the useful range isn't squeezed into a tiny sliver at one end.

Recording vs. artist

You can tune a specific recording (just this one song) or an artist (everything credited to them). Both apply at once and multiply together — turning an artist's Preference down quiets everything of theirs, even a specific song of theirs you've separately turned up.

Reset, Cancel, Save

  • Reset (next to each individual dial) — clears just that one field back to "use the default," discarding anything you'd set for it.
  • Cancel — closes the panel without saving anything you changed.
  • Save — applies whatever you've changed. Fields you never touched are left exactly as they were.

Play frequency

Underneath the preference panel, a small table fills in on its own (it never delays the panel opening) showing how often this recording or artist has actually played: across the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 365 days, and all time. The All row is every play for any reason. The User row is plays you personally queued or started. Any other rows are plays chosen automatically, broken out by which scheduled program was active when it happened — see Advanced Features for what a "program" is. Older listening history recorded before this breakdown existed only ever counts toward All, which is why All can show more than every other row put together, but never less. This table closes automatically when you close the preference panel.
