Quick Start

Lempi is already set up: it has music, it knows what it is, and it has a schedule telling it what to play at different times of day. Turn it on and it plays — this page is for everything else you might want to do.

Finding this page again

Running on your own computer? It's at http://localhost:5720/ (or whatever port you started it on). Running on a Raspberry Pi appliance elsewhere on your network? You need its address — see Starting From Nothing's "If Lempi runs on a Raspberry Pi appliance" section for exactly how to find it and get it onto your Wi-Fi in the first place. Either way, once you have the address, bookmark it — Lempi keeps running and keeps the same address between visits.

The basics

  • Play / Pause / Skip — three buttons, always in the same place. Skip moves straight to whatever's queued next.
  • Volume — the slider. It only sends your change once you let go of it, so dragging around doesn't spam the player.
  • Seeking — on most skins, click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that point in the current track.

What's playing, and what's next

The now-playing area shows the title and artist of whatever's sounding right now, usually with cover art. Below or beside it is the queue — the handful of tracks lined up to play next. Each queued row has small controls to move it sooner, move it later, or remove it outright — nothing you queue is locked in until it actually plays.

Finding something to hear

Click Browse to look through the whole library by artist, album, or track. Type in the search box to narrow it down, or use the A–Z bar to jump straight to a letter. Tap a track to select it (or several), then choose Now, Next, or Last to queue it exactly where you want it. You never have to queue anything at all, though — left alone, Lempi keeps itself supplied automatically, choosing what plays according to its own schedule and whatever it's learned about your listening. (More on how that works in the Appendix.)

Which "skin" am I looking at?

Lempi's web page can look quite different depending on which skin you (or your browser) picked — a picker in the footer of every page lets you switch between them any time. The skins are not just different colors: some features (like Play History and Bluetooth speaker settings) only exist in one of them. If you're looking for something described elsewhere in this guide and can't find it, try switching skins before assuming it isn't there — see the capability table in Advanced Features for exactly what each skin offers. A browser that has never chosen a skin starts on MuLibPlay, which is the plainest of the three — if you want everything Lempi can do, try switching to the Lempi skin itself.

Tuning what you hear

See a title or artist name that's underlined? Click it. That opens a small panel where you can tell Lempi to play that song or artist more often, less often, or leave it alone — see Preference Tuning for the details. You don't have to touch any of this; it's here for when you want to nudge things.
