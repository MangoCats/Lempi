# Review: documentation against implementation, and the open gaps (2026-10-14)

A review of the repository at `02e8509`: what the documentation says against
what the code does, the known gaps and debt gathered in one place, and which
of them matter most. It follows the review of 2026-10-08 (the two untracked
`Review-8Oct26.md` and `OpusReview-8Oct26.md` in the maintainer's checkout)
and says which of its findings still hold. Nothing in the tree was changed
by the review itself.

**Method.** The gates were run on the hub desktop; the 2026-10-08 findings
were re-checked against the code and the commits since; every document's
"Open" section, every "not yet built", "deferred" and "Today …" claim was read
against the code it names; the remaining gaps were checked in the code, not
taken from the documents' word.

## 3. Known gaps, documented future work, debt

**Fleet and sync.** `lempi02w` (`340be21`, 2026-10-10; this page first said
`9ff6e0d4a046`, from a paragraph older than [FLEET001](../fleet/FLEET001-the-fleet.md)'s table) and `teacherslounge` are not on
the current build; `lempi02w` still credits Woodstock's and *An American
Treasure*'s second discs as their first discs' songs until its next sync.
Rolling a signed commit back from the Sync page `[SPEC-STAR-920]`; names for
unresolved items `[SPEC-STAR-930]`; the check's time on the smallest node
`[SPEC-NKP-900]`.

**Selection.** A passage that crashed the player comes straight back
`[SPEC-DIR-240]`; a restored queue is not re-weighed and loses who chose each
entry `[SPEC-DIR-235]`, `[SPEC-DIR-245]`; `switch::carry_queue` discards the
drops waiting before a handoff `[REQ-PD-112]`; eligibility is judged at
selection, not play time `[SPEC-DIR-210]`; likes never age `[SPEC-DIR-215]`;
`flavor.accuracy` is NULL on all 578,452 rows, so the distance metric's
accuracy weighting has never run `[SPEC-PL-100]`.

**Restore.** Only the play history is re-pointed through a renumbering;
`player_state` and `player_queue` are not, so after a rebuild the resume point
and the remembered queue can name other songs `[REQ-LIB-160]`.

**Playback and UI.** The now-playing panel is blank while MPD plays
([architecture.md](../docs/architecture.md) §10); how a seek feels is a
question for the ear; there is no whole-album play, so `[REQ-PD-127]` has
nothing to act on, and the passage page shows no hold `[SPEC-HOLD-900]`; the
default skin and the settings layout are deferred by decision.

**Library and ingest.** Multi-disc sets and hidden audio as passages, designed
and not built ([SPEC026](../docs/spec/SPEC026-cd-ripping-passages.md)); the
automatic MusicBrainz edition search for a DAO split `[SPEC-SA-126]`; the
console's per-file identity vocabulary `[SPEC-SUI-055]` and a re-read of kept
payloads after an upgrade `[SPEC-SUI-175]`; relink's candidate tier
([SPEC012](../docs/spec/SPEC012-library-relink.md)); embedded tag writing
([SPEC006](../docs/spec/SPEC006-data-flow-and-portability.md)); the artist
folder is not held to the album-name length `[SPEC-NAM-900]`.

**Code health.** Errors that read as zero in the census, the pool count and
other figures shown to a person (O-04); the `Engine`'s 70 fields (M-01);
tests at the boundaries (M-03).

**Appliances.** A reopened Bluetooth stream still needs a run of ten minutes
or more before it is trusted (ROADMAP §4); a Wi-Fi watchdog `[FLT-ISS-015]`;
the private fleet repository `[SPEC-FCP-010]`.

**Undecided, product.** Multi-user and authentication; a kiosk display;
station identification, announcements and smart-home hooks; peer mesh against
the star; browse pagination (ROADMAP §3).

## 4. The most impactful, in order

1. **C1, a phone's credit corrections dropped silently** -- silent damage to a
   client's catalogue, and a report of success that did not happen (CLAUDE.md
   §6). Apply credits to a held file on import, or acknowledge only what landed.
2. **`lempi02w` and `teacherslounge` onto the current build, and synced** --
   `lempi02w` is where most listening edits are made, and plays wrong titles
   now; its next sync corrects itself `[SPEC-STAR-945]`.
3. **A passage that crashes the player comes straight back** `[SPEC-DIR-240]` --
   a cheap guard, worth most on appliances powered by their speaker.
4. **Restore re-points the resume point and the queue** `[REQ-LIB-160]` -- the
   operation used under stress.
5. **Errors that read as zero** in figures shown to a person (O-04) -- the
   failure mode behind several past incidents.
6. **A sweep of C2 to C9**, and one gap register instead of two -- cheap, and
   it removes the stale claims that misled this very review.
7. **`carry_queue`'s drops** `[REQ-PD-112]` -- small and understood.
8. **Multi-disc sets as one album** (SPEC026) -- 38 confirmed sets in the
   library, and four of this month's imports.
9. **`flavor.accuracy`** `[SPEC-PL-100]` -- selection quality across the whole
   library, once the model manifest carries it.
10. **A restored queue re-weighed, its provenance kept** `[SPEC-DIR-235]`, `[SPEC-DIR-245]`.
11. **CI visible from the desktop**, and the two root review files filed or retired (C10).
